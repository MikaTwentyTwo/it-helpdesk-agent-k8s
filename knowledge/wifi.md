# Corporate network troubleshooting
Ask which device lost Wi-Fi and whether colleagues are affected.
Check whether Wi-Fi is enabled on the user's device and whether the correct corporate SSID is selected.
Do not reboot shared routers or change DNS, IP, VLAN or security settings without administrator approval.
Different devices normally have different IP addresses. Equal addresses may indicate a conflict.
A network printer does not necessarily require internet access, but it needs reachable local network connectivity.
Indicator meanings depend on the device manual. Do not infer network health from color or steady light alone.
Do not assume that a printer connects directly to a computer by Ethernet.
Escalate a shared network outage to authorized IT staff.
